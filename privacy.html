Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy describes how the operator of Rclone Drive Migration handles information when using the rclone command-line program to copy selected Google Drive files and folders between Google accounts.

In brief: This website is informational only. It does not collect Google account data or receive Drive files. The migration is run locally by the operator on their own computer.

2. Information Accessed

When the operator authorizes rclone to access a Google Drive account, rclone may access Drive files, folders, and metadata permitted by the OAuth permissions selected during setup.

This information is used only to carry out the file-copy operation requested by the operator and to check or report the result.

3. How Information Is Used

Google Drive information is used to identify selected source files and folders, copy them to the selected destination account, and optionally compare source and destination contents.

Information is not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

4. Storage and Security

OAuth tokens and rclone configuration are stored locally on the operator's computer in the rclone configuration file, unless another storage location is deliberately configured.

Transfer and verification logs may also be stored locally. Depending on logging settings, logs may contain file paths, names, timestamps, and error details.

The operator is responsible for protecting the computer, rclone configuration file, OAuth credentials, and logs from unauthorized access.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

This website does not receive or share Google Drive content. During a migration, rclone communicates with Google services to perform operations authorized by the account holder.

Information may therefore be processed by Google under Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. The operator does not sell Google user data or disclose it to third parties for advertising.

6. Retention and Deletion

Files copied to the destination remain there until the destination account owner deletes them.

Local OAuth tokens, configuration files, and logs remain on the operator's computer until the operator deletes them. The operator can revoke application access through Google Account security settings.

7. Website Data

This is a static informational website. It does not include a sign-in form, analytics, advertising scripts, or a mechanism for uploading files.

GitHub Pages may process standard technical request information as part of hosting and delivering this website, according to GitHub's own policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the migration workflow or its data practices change.
